🔬 Species Guide

  • Ixodes ricinus: Primary Lyme disease vector found in woodlands.
  • Ixodes hexagonus: Hedgehog tick, commonly found on garden pets.
  • Dermacentor reticulatus: Ornate dog vector, expanding across the UK.

🛡️ Prevention Tips

  • • Use veterinarian-approved year-round tick preventatives.
  • • Check pets thoroughly after walks through tall grass or bracken.
  • • Safely remove attached ticks immediately using a twist tool.
